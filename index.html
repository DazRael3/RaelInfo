How estimates work

Calculator outputs are generated in your browser from the assumptions you enter. Market and reference-rate panels use external public data services and may be delayed, unavailable or revised. Confirm important decisions with a qualified professional and the relevant provider.

Your data and privacy

This version does not create an account or transmit calculator inputs to a RaelInfo server. Optional saved scenarios use this browser's local storage; export a JSON backup to move data between devices. Public market-data requests are sent to their respective providers. Avoid using a shared device for sensitive financial scenarios.

Important information: RaelInfo provides general educational estimates, not financial, investment, legal, mortgage, tax or accounting advice. Results are simplified and may omit fees, taxes, insurance requirements, changing rates, lender rules, transaction costs and individual circumstances. Crypto prices are indicative market data and can be volatile. Check assumptions and source data before acting. Nothing on this site is an offer to buy or sell a financial product.
