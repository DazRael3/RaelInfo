Reference rates versus money-transfer quotes

A daily reference rate is not necessarily the rate a customer receives from a bank, payment card, exchange desk or money-transfer provider. Providers may apply a spread, a fixed fee, a percentage fee, a minimum charge or other conditions. To compare a transfer, compare the final amount the recipient receives after all fees.

The data on this page is provided by Frankfurter, which aggregates reference exchange rates from official sources. Rate dates may be earlier than the current date, particularly on weekends and holidays.

Cross-border planning checklist

  • Compare the receiving amount, not just the advertised exchange rate.
  • Check whether the provider charges a separate fee or includes its margin in the rate.
  • Confirm supported currencies, transfer limits, delivery timing and eligibility.
  • For recurring transfers, compare the cost over several typical amounts.
