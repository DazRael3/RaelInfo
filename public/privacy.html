Privacy Notice

This page describes the data behavior of the RaelInfo website, which uses Cloudflare Pages/Pages Functions, Cloudflare D1, and Supabase Auth in production. The site operator must review it against the actual hosting configuration, legal entity, third-party services and applicable law. This template is not legal advice.

1. Calculator inputs

Calculations run in the visitor's browser. Calculator balances, income, debt amounts and other calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to RaelInfo API endpoints. When a visitor looks up a stock symbol, news topic, or SEC CIK, that query is sent to the RaelInfo server and then to the relevant public or configured data provider. Do not enter sensitive information on a shared or public device.

2. Local browser storage

RaelInfo can store calculator values and display preferences in the browser's local storage so a visitor can return to a scenario. This data is not encrypted by the application, is not synced between devices and may be accessible to anyone with access to the browser profile. The JSON export feature creates a file on the visitor's device. Visitors should protect exported files as they would other personal financial records. A clear-data control is provided in the application.

3. Public data services

The website requests indicative crypto-market data from CoinLore, daily currency reference-rate data from Frankfurter, official financial and economic announcement feeds from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the Federal Reserve Board and the Bank of Canada, and (when configured) stock data from Alpha Vantage, Financial Modeling Prep or EOD Historical Data. SEC filing metadata is also requested from data.sec.gov when the operator has configured the required identifying SEC User-Agent. News searches are processed by the RaelInfo server against cached official publisher feeds; the search phrase is not sent to those publishers. These requests do not include the amounts entered in calculators. The providers and network infrastructure may process technical connection information (such as IP address, request metadata and user-agent data) under their own policies.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

RaelInfo uses browser local storage for preferences and optional calculator scenarios. Optional accounts use an email address and password. Supabase Auth manages password credentials, email confirmation and authentication sessions; RaelInfo does not store raw passwords. The Pages Functions use HttpOnly cookies for Supabase access and refresh tokens, plus a separate CSRF token for state-changing requests. Supabase Auth is a third-party service provider and processes account information under its own privacy terms. The operator may enable Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate page and performance metrics; Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first and says it does not collect or use visitors’ personal data. RaelInfo also records aggregate daily counts for calculator-use categories, scenario export/import, print actions and Pro interest joins. These event records are aggregated by date, event type, category and market and are not linked to a user account. A separate Pro interest record is linked to the authenticated account ID, as described below. These event records do not contain calculator amounts, balances, portfolio holdings or user identifiers. API rate limiting stores hashed IP-derived bucket keys in D1 for request control and periodically attempts to remove entries older than 48 hours; raw IP addresses are not stored in that rate-limit table. If advertising or other tracking integrations are added later, this notice and any required consent settings must be reviewed before activation.

5. Affiliate links and click measurement

No affiliate offers are active unless the operator configures a program after approval and confirms that public website promotion is permitted. Each active offer is displayed only for its configured market and calculator category and includes a disclosure beside the link. When a visitor chooses an offer, the request passes through a RaelInfo redirect endpoint that records the offer ID, partner name, selected market, calculator category, placement and timestamp before redirecting to the provider. These click records do not contain calculator inputs, portfolio details, customer names or email addresses. They are outbound redirect counts, not unique people or confirmed sales. Third-party destinations may use their own cookies and tracking technologies under their own privacy notices.

6. Hosting, partner inquiries and security

Cloudflare Pages Functions receive API requests for authentication, stock lookups, news searches, SEC filing lookups, portfolio actions, affiliate redirects and reporting. Supabase Auth processes email/password authentication. Cloudflare D1 stores the RaelInfo account profile, portfolio positions, aggregate event counts, affiliate click records, product-interest records and business partnership inquiries. When business inquiries are enabled by the operator, the partnership form collects the contact name, business email, organization, optional company website, inquiry type and message so the operator can review and respond. These inquiries are visible only in the token-protected owner dashboard; the form does not currently send an automatic email acknowledgement. A RaelInfo Pro interest record is linked to the authenticated account ID and is not a paid subscription or an email-marketing opt-in. Market-data requests may be forwarded to CoinLore, Frankfurter, the SEC, the Federal Reserve, the Bank of Canada, SEC EDGAR and any configured stock-data provider. Official news feeds are retrieved and cached by publisher; visitor search phrases are matched locally against cached article metadata. API keys and owner-dashboard tokens are stored as server-side Cloudflare secrets and are not included in static frontend files. Providers and network infrastructure may process IP addresses, query parameters and technical request metadata under their own terms and privacy notices. The operator should establish and apply retention and deletion procedures for business inquiries and revenue records before public launch.

7. Retention and deletion

Calculator scenarios stored in local storage remain on the visitor’s device until removed by the visitor or browser. Portfolio positions are stored in Cloudflare D1 and associated with the authenticated Supabase user ID. Supabase Auth manages credentials and sessions separately. Signed-in users can request account and portfolio deletion from the dashboard when the operator has enabled the required Supabase administrative secret. Pro interest can be withdrawn from the dashboard. Business inquiry contact details and manually entered affiliate conversion records are retained in D1 for the operator’s business review and recordkeeping; the operator must define and follow a reasonable retention schedule and delete them when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements. D1 retention, backups and deletion behavior are also subject to Cloudflare settings and operator procedures. A monitored privacy contact must be published for privacy requests.

8. Children

The site is intended as a general financial-information resource and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The operator should adapt this section if the intended audience or features change.

9. Operator and contact

RaelInfo is independently operated from Ontario, Canada. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@raelinfo.com. The operator will review and respond to privacy inquiries. The legal identity of the operator, applicable privacy jurisdiction, and any additional contact or representative information required by law remain subject to review before public launch. This starter notice is not a final privacy policy.

10. Changes

This notice should be updated whenever the site's data practices, integrations, hosting, or legal obligations change. Display the actual effective date after review.
