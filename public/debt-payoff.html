Why the payment must exceed monthly interest

At a constant APR, a simplified monthly interest estimate is the balance multiplied by the annual rate divided by 12. If the planned payment is no greater than the interest charged that month, the balance will not decline under this model.

Actual card issuers may calculate interest daily, apply payments to balances with different APRs, change minimum payments, add fees or change rates. Check the terms of each account.

Snowball and avalanche planning

The single-debt estimator above does not decide how to prioritize multiple accounts. RaelInfo's main debt planner allows up to three balances with separate APRs and a monthly budget. The snowball approach prioritizes smaller balances; the avalanche approach prioritizes higher interest rates. Compare the estimated interest and payoff path, and verify how your lender applies payments.
