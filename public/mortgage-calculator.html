How the mortgage estimate works

For a fixed-rate loan with monthly payments, the principal-and-interest payment uses the standard amortization formula:

M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n)

  • P is the loan principal.
  • r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12).
  • n is the total number of monthly payments.

When the interest rate is zero, the principal is divided evenly over the payment count. Property tax and insurance are added as simple monthly averages.

What this estimate leaves out

This is not a lender quote or loan approval. It does not model mortgage insurance, mortgage default insurance, escrow timing, HOA or condo fees, closing costs, down-payment rules, points, adjustable rates, prepayment options or local lending rules. Canadian mortgage compounding conventions and US loan structures can differ; confirm the exact payment convention with the lender.

Use the result to compare scenarios, then confirm all fees, taxes and insurance with the relevant provider or qualified professional.
