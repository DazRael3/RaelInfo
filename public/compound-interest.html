What compounding means

Compounding means that growth can be earned on both the original balance and prior growth. With recurring contributions, each deposit has its own amount of time to compound. This calculator uses a monthly equivalent of the entered annual return and assumes each contribution is made at the end of the month.

The result is a mathematical scenario, not an investment forecast. Actual returns are uneven, may be negative, and can be reduced by fees, taxes and inflation.

How to interpret the result

  • Compare a conservative, middle and optimistic return assumption instead of relying on one number.
  • Test what happens if contributions pause or begin later.
  • Review the estimated contributions separately from estimated growth.
  • Do not treat a smooth projection as a promise of investment performance.
